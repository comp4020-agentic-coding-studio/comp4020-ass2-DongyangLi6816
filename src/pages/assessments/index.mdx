Five pieces of work. One is marked every week, for being in the room with
what the stand-up asked you to bring. Two are individual and written. One is
held in the room, on someone else's work, under the course's own calibration
rules, and marked on the page you write afterwards, not on the three
minutes. The last turns the course around: you design the review system,
and the class spends a week trying to beat it.

Each piece says what to write, what to submit and by when, what the marker
checks, and what each grade looks like.
