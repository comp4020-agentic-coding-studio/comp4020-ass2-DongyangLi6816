Five pieces of work. One is marked every week, for being in the room with
what the stand-up asked you to bring. One is your own self-review, in two
registers. One is a manager's case for an engineer whose packet you were
handed by lottery. One is the meeting where your group fits those cases to
a curve, marked on the page you write afterwards, not on your two minutes.
The last turns the course around: you design the review system for that
team, and your group spends a week trying to beat it.

Each piece says what to write, what to submit and by when, what the marker
checks, and what each grade looks like.
