Three rules and one honest paragraph. The first two are the course's own
system applied to you, which the brief for every assessment says out loud.
The third is where the course drops the act.

## Calibration

Marks in this course are calibrated. Your tutor proposes a mark for each
piece of work. The teaching team then meets without you and fits the proposals
to the distribution the school expects. You will not be told what the
distribution is. You will be told your mark.

This is how it works where you are going. We would be lying to run it any
other way, and the course is about the lie.

Most of you will meet expectations.

## Visibility

Work is submitted in the course forum. Work that is not in the forum by the
due time does not exist for marking purposes, however good it was and whoever
you emailed it to.

This is week 5 as a rule. You are welcome to find it unfair. Week 12 is where
you get to design something fairer.

## Extensions

Extensions are easy to get and there is no trick to them. If something has
happened, message the convenor before the due time, say roughly what and how
long you need, and you will get it. You do not need a certificate for anything
under a week. Nothing about an extension is calibrated, recorded against you,
or remembered by anyone in a room.

## Getting help

The stand-up is the place to ask. The forum is the second place. Direct
messages to the tutor are read on Fridays, which is a lesson, but they are
read.

If the course itself is the problem, the week 10 material in particular, tell
the convenor. It has happened before and the answer is not a rubric line.
