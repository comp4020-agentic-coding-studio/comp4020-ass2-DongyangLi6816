Twelve engineers on one team at a company that runs a curve. Their manager
moved teams in the April reorg. You are the new manager, and these packets
are all you have: a self-review, two peer paragraphs, three notes the old
manager left, the numbers, the announcement if there was one, and where the
person sits.

Every packet is a real quarter as the room would see it. Most of them have
something in them that this course has a week for. Some of them have a
person in them that the numbers cannot see. Which is which is Assessment 2.
