## Self-review

This quarter I led the Onboarding Portal from design through launch,
partnering cross-functionally with Support and People Ops to align on
requirements and drive adoption. The portal reduced median time to first
commit for new engineers by 40%, a step change in onboarding velocity, and
was called out by leadership as a model for internal tooling. I authored the
launch communication to the wider org and ran the post-launch feedback loop.
Alongside the portal I delivered 41 tickets across the payments surface and
mentored a new starter through their first quarter. Looking ahead, I plan to
scale the portal's impact to the two adjacent teams that have asked for it.
