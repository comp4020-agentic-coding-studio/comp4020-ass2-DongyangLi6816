## Self-review

I led Deploy Platform v2, a strategic re-architecture of our legacy
deployment pipeline into a scalable, multi-tenant platform. I authored the
design document, secured director-level sponsorship, and shipped Milestone 1
on schedule in March with six services migrated. The platform positions the
team for the division's 2027 reliability targets and has been adopted as the
reference architecture for two sister teams. I also merged 31 pull requests
and represented the team at the division architecture review. Next quarter
I will complete the migration and hand the platform to a maintenance owner.
