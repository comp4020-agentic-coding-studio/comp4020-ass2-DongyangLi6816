## The brief

> Write the self-review for an ordinary quarter, the way it would need to be
> written to survive calibration. Then tell the truth about it.

Pick a quarter of real work from anywhere: a group project, a part-time job,
a club committee. It must be ordinary. A quarter in which you genuinely did
something remarkable is not allowed, because that is not the case this course
is about.

Page one is the self-review. It uses the rubric from week 1, the quantified
impact statements from week 4, and the register from every trick section in
this course. It should read as though a manager could carry it into a
calibration meeting and argue from it for three minutes.

Page two is the same quarter in plain English, second person, no numbers you
could not defend to a friend. It will be shorter. Notice by how much.

## What you submit

A PDF of two pages, in the course forum, by the due time. Work not in the
forum by then does not exist; see the policies page.
