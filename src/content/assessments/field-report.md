Set in week 8. Due at 5 pm on Friday of week 9.

## The brief

> Find one trick from this course being used by someone who has not done
> this course. Explain why it works. Say what would stop it.

The setting can be anything with a rating in it: a group project, a
workplace, a sports team, a share house with a chore chart. You are looking
for the skip-level coffee, the public post, the launch email, the rebuild,
the scheduled message, or the single point of failure. Once you know the
shapes you will see them everywhere; pick one and describe it precisely.

Then the harder two parts. Name the mechanism from the lecture it exploits,
and explain it as if to the person running the system, who has never heard
of Goodhart. Then propose the change that would stop the trick, and say
honestly what it would cost them. A fix with no cost is a sign you have not
found the mechanism.

## What you submit

Up to 800 words, in the course forum, by the due time. Your report is the
document a classmate will argue for in the week 11 calibration, so write it
for a stranger who has three minutes.
