Julia Evans's advice is to keep a running document of what you did, so that
when the review comes your manager can make your case from it and a new
manager can learn what you did before they arrived. It is good advice. It is
also the raw material of the self-review, which is the one document you write
that reaches the calibration room.

In January 2026 Amazon's review process began requiring corporate employees to
list three to five accomplishments demonstrating impact, feeding directly into
compensation. That is the self-review with the pretence removed: a short list,
summarised again by the manager, summarised again by the chair. Each summary
keeps what is easy to carry, and what is easy to carry is a number. This
lecture is about what that does to the truth of the document, and why the
honest version so often loses to the quantified one.
