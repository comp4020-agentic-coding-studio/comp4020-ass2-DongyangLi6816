This is the week of Assessment 3. The room is set up the way week 1 described:
groups of eight, a tutor in the chair, two "exceeds" slots, one "below" to be
filled. You argue for someone else's field report. They argue for yours.

## Bring

The field report you have been assigned to argue for. You had it since
Monday. Read it twice.

## The trick

Recognise saturation. Once every person on a team splits their tickets, posts
in public and writes their own launch email, those behaviours stop
differentiating anyone and become the cost of standing still. The rational
response is to change teams, or companies, before the cycle in which the
saturated behaviours are re-baselined as expected.

## What happens

The calibration runs for forty minutes. Then, with the ratings fixed, the
group spends twenty minutes explaining the outcome without reference to the
quality of the reports: who spoke first, who the chair knew, which report had
a number in its first line. The explanations are usually complete. Then one
last question for the room: who would have left before this cycle if they
could have? That is who pays for this week's trick, and it is everyone who
stayed.

## How you know it worked

You have been calibrated, you have calibrated someone, and you can account for
the result using only what you learned in weeks 1 to 8. If that feels bad, it
is meant to; week 12 is where you fix it.
