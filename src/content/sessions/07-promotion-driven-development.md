First stand-up after the break. Weeks 2 and 6 taught you that the measure is
not the work and that credit follows the announcement. Put those together and
you get the incentive that shapes most large software companies.

## Bring

Something you rely on that works and that nobody is praised for: a script, a
shared spreadsheet, a rota, a piece of infrastructure.

## The trick

Identify a working system with an unclear owner and propose a next-generation
replacement. Frame the existing system as legacy, the replacement as a
platform, and the migration as a strategic investment. Ship the first
milestone before the review cycle closes. Impact is assessed at launch;
migration costs are assessed in someone else's review.

## What happens

You write the one-page proposal to replace the thing you brought. It has to be
convincing. Then you fill in a second page the proposal does not have: who has
to migrate, what breaks, and which quarter each cost lands in. The group reads
the first page as a manager and the second page as the person on call.

## How you know it worked

You can look at a shiny replacement for a working thing and say whose review
it was built for. Assessment 4 is set this week; read the brief before next
stand-up.
