Week 5 was about work being seen. This week is about the appearance of work,
which is rated separately and is much cheaper to produce.

## Bring

Your calendar for last week, and the time of day of your last ten sent
messages.

## The trick

Signal engagement through observable proxies. Send the summary email late,
scheduled rather than written late. Decline no meetings. Block "focus time" in
the shared calendar and post about the outcome. Every proxy is a low-cost
signal that reads as commitment in the absence of anything better to read.

## What happens

You list five things about you that a manager could see without seeing your
work. Then, in the room, you manufacture one of them: a scheduled message, a
calendar block, a status. It takes about ninety seconds. Then the harder part:
you say which of the five you have caught yourself using to judge someone
else.

## How you know it worked

You can distinguish a signal of effort from effort, and you have noticed that
you cannot always do it for other people. Assessment 2 is set this week.
