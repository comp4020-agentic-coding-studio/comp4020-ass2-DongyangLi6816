The first stand-up of the block where things stop working. Weeks 3 to 8 gave
you tricks that depend on people remembering you. This week, the people
change.

## Bring

Your org chart from week 3 and your brag document from week 4.

## The trick

Concentrate irreplaceable context. Be the sole owner of a system the
organisation depends on and that nobody else has had time to learn. In a
restructure, the retained roles are the ones whose removal breaks something
visible. Documentation is a strategic decision; make it deliberately.

## What happens

You invent a reorg: your manager moves, your team is merged, your skip-level
is now someone from another building. You redraw the chart. Then you go
through every trick from weeks 3 to 8 and mark whether it survives. The
skip-level coffee does not. The public posts do, partly. The launch email
does, if the new manager reads old email. The brag document survives only if
it was written for a stranger.

## How you know it worked

You can say what a reorg does to each trick you know, and you have noticed
that the one that survives best is the one that makes you a risk to remove,
and therefore a risk to move anywhere else.
Assessment 2, the case for an engineer whose manager left in exactly this
kind of reorg, is due on Friday.
